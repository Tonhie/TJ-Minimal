# Xu Beihong and His Horses
## Zhao Leyou | Academy of Fine Arts

---

# Xu Beihong’s Life

- **Early Years**: Born in 1895 in Jiangsu, China.
- **International Education**: Studied art in France, mastering the techniques of Western oil painting.
- **Artistic Mission**: Returned to China to serve as an art teacher, dedicated to modernizing and improving Chinese art.

---

# His Artistic Style

> "He mixed Chinese ink brush with Western realism."

- **Synthesis**: A unique blend of traditional ink techniques and realistic anatomy.
- **Aesthetics**: His style is characterized as powerful, simple, and evocative.
- **Technique**: Focused on strong lines and clear shapes rather than intricate, ornamental details.

---

# The Features of His Horses

<div class="ldiv">

- **Vivid Motion**: His horses appear alive, captured in mid-movement.
- **Dynamic Anatomy**: 
    - Strong legs
    - Flying manes
    - Bright, spirited eyes

</div>
<div class="rdiv">

- **Brushwork**: 
    - Thick black ink defines the body.
    - Quick, sweeping brushstrokes suggest incredible speed.
- **Symbolism**: These horses represent the pursuit of **freedom** and the **unyielding spirit** of China.

</div>
